## Apple: product first

Inspected the rendered hero and scrolled feature galleries at https://www.apple.com/os/macos/.

The opening uses centered, substantial type above a moving product demonstration. Motion has a visible pause control. Below, the background becomes pale gray; large rounded screenshots dominate, with short captions underneath. A horizontally cropped next item makes the gallery discoverable. Navigation stays in a compact translucent strip. Captions start with a darker descriptive phrase and continue in quieter text. Availability appears separately from the headline.

Apply to V1: the mechanism is the product. Give the running experiment most of the opening surface, keep its explanation beside the result, and make pause obvious. Use generous feature stages and real demonstration previews instead of repeating text cards. Keep limitations nearby without burying the interaction. Do not imitate proprietary artwork, product silhouettes, or Apple’s promotional claims.
