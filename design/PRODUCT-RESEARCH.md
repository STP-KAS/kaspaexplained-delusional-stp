## Changes to prioritize

1. Make each application's first view an actual transaction object: roles, amount and next allowed action. Put wallet setup alongside this context instead of making an unexplained wallet the entire first screen.
2. Replace generic status paragraphs with one compact current-state card and a single primary next action. Keep recovery/check actions available beside the transaction they concern.
3. Present principal, funding fee, maximum exit fee and estimated return before locking coins. The proof example especially needs its high verification cost visible at this stage.
4. Add explicit role ownership/authority: buyer, seller, arbiter; the two selected treasury signers; prediction oracle. This demo holds all role keys—show that fact plainly.
5. Separate plain-language review from inspectable data. Amounts, recipients, network and fees stay visible; scripts, hashes, JSON and mass remain available in details.

## Access limits and evidence boundaries

Safe's actual signing, transaction queue and execution were not exercised: no watched account or wallet connection was added. Its transaction-state observations come from the official embedded screenshots. Uniswap's quote/picker were exercised without connecting; wallet approval, fee confirmation and final swap were not tested. Polymarket's public selectors, market detail and order-book disclosure were inspected; authentication and trading were not completed. Rainbow/Solflare observations are official help-page and screenshot observations, not device tests. Live prices, probabilities and promotional wording are not adopted as product or factual claims for this site.

## Priority expansion: major wallet products

The design priority is Safe, Uniswap, Polymarket, MetaMask and Phantom. Rainbow/Solflare are supplementary recovery/receive references. This priority follows the user's requested recognition/usage weighting; no quantitative user-count, market-cap, or market-share ranking was established by this review. Wallet products do not have a directly comparable market capitalization in every case, so token capitalization is not used as a proxy for wallet usage.

- **MetaMask actual application:** opened [Portfolio](https://portfolio.metamask.io/), dismissed the privacy gate through its settings route, expanded Move crypto, and opened [Receive](https://portfolio.metamask.io/transfer?tab=receive). The rendered receive panel contains Send/Transfer/Receive tabs, a clearly labeled recipient wallet selector and a single Connect wallet action. The selector is disabled until a wallet is available. Screenshot inspected. No wallet connected, so address/QR display was not tested. Apply this task-shaped panel: let the user choose what to do, then present exactly the prerequisite needed for that action. Our Receive test coins should identify the disposable wallet, distinct from a contract deposit.
- **MetaMask recovery:** opened [official restore documentation](https://support.metamask.io/configure/wallet/how-to-restore-your-metamask-wallet-from-secret-recovery-phrase), expanded the Extension disclosure and inspected its embedded onboarding screenshots. Existing-wallet import is a first-class choice; the import screenshot shows a numbered two-step process, masked recovery entries, and explicit reveal/clear controls. The article distinguishes restoring the base wallet from manually imported accounts. Apply first-class Restore alongside Create, clear file/password stages, and confirmation that all three role keys and the exact contract were restored. Do not imply restoring a single payer key recovers every contract role.
- **Phantom recovery:** opened [official recovery-phrase creation guidance](https://help.phantom.com/articles/45135465489555) and inspected its rendered page. It distinguishes the browser unlock password from the recovery phrase and requires explicit acknowledgement of saved recovery. Apply equally precise wording to our different model: the recovery password decrypts the exported file, and both are needed; it is not a resettable website login. This is documentation evidence, not an installed Phantom onboarding test.

These additions reinforce the main changes rather than adding more surface area: one recognizable wallet/action panel, clear amount movement, staged recovery, explicit ownership and a durable receipt. No additional visual style is imported merely because a product is prominent.
