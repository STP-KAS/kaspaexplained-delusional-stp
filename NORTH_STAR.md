# Kaspa Explained

An independent home for understanding and participating in Kaspa. A visitor should recognize the network, find a useful next step, and understand its mechanics without completing a course.

## What the site must answer

- What is Kaspa, and what is KAS used for?
- How do wallets, payments, fees, and confirmation work?
- What changes when proof-of-work blocks can arrive in parallel?
- What exists today, and what is still being developed?
- How can I build, mine, verify, or learn more?
- What risks and tradeoffs should affect my decision?

Use these questions to decide what earns space. Keep specialist material available through specific labels and primary sources. Do not hide limitations needed to understand the main claim.

## Experience

Lead with the network and useful actions. Let one working visual explain the central difference. Demos belong beside their subjects; the demo directory should also offer something to try immediately.

Show a meaningful state before interaction. Controls must reveal a consequence, not create work for its own sake. Keep replay, keyboard access, reduced motion, and small screens usable. An illustration must not imply it runs production consensus or measures live data.

Use familiar crypto terms when precise: wallet, KAS, fee, mining, node, proof of work, covenant. Explain an unfamiliar term when needed. No inflated adoption claims, price promises, or artificial excitement.

## Design

Strong type, recognizable Kaspa identity, a restrained green accent, generous spacing, and tangible demonstration surfaces. A useful home, not a dashboard of unexplained metrics or a textbook index. Light and dark should both feel considered. Preserve the existing mark and protected books.

Keep routes for learning, payments, network status, building, mining, history, risks, and experiments easy to find. The guide is optional navigation, not a compulsory sequence. Posts are a separate editorial product.

## Interaction references · September 6, 2026

The xemx public site, deposition explorer, and material studies informed the shared scene, control, and readout structure. Their materials vocabulary and branding do not belong here. The propagation example has one independent condition: message delay. Time scrubbing inspects its consequences; replay is optional.

[Bartosz Ciechanowski's mechanical explanations](https://ciechanow.ski/mechanical-watch/) and [Red Blob Games' pathfinding explanations](https://www.redblobgames.com/pathfinding/a-star/introduction.html) informed the use of direct manipulation beside a specific explanation. These are interaction references, not evidence about Kaspa or proof of learning outcomes.

Review three independent journeys: a newcomer understanding a payment and parallel blocks; a technical reader reaching assumptions and implementation details directly; and an existing user finding wallets, transaction inspection, node information, and current tool status. A successful walkthrough must not make any of them complete the other two journeys first.

## Industry comparison · September 5, 2026

Primary-site review informed this direction:

- [Ethereum](https://ethereum.org/) and [Bitcoin.org](https://bitcoin.org/en/): identity, wallets, use, and building before deep mechanics.
- [Solana](https://solana.com/) and [Avalanche](https://www.avax.network/): current activity, concrete applications, and distinct participation paths.
- [XRPL](https://xrpl.org/): clear use categories and visible availability distinctions.
- [Sui](https://www.sui.io/), [BNB Chain](https://www.bnbchain.org/en), [Cardano](https://cardano.org/), and [NEAR](https://www.near.org/): recognizable positioning followed by products, tools, or participation.
- [Polkadot's starting guide](https://wiki.polkadot.com/general/getting-started/): wallets, apps, learning, safety, and community. Its homepage was not readable in the research tool.
- [Coinbase Learn](https://www.coinbase.com/learn/crypto-basics): direct questions and approachable topic discovery.

These are observed publishing patterns, not user-testing evidence. They inform the structure; they do not prove what every visitor wants. Competitor claims are not evidence for Kaspa facts. Do not copy their assets, slogans, or unverified statistics.
